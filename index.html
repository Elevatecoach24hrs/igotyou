Need someone now?

Supporter guide

Someone you love is struggling. Here's what helps.

1. Stay calm and stay with them

You don't need the perfect words. Being there is what matters. Call them back, go round, or stay on the phone.

2. Listen more than you talk

Let them say it. Try "I'm here. Tell me what's going on." Don't rush to fix it, judge it, or say "cheer up".

3. Ask them straight

"Are you thinking about suicide?" Asking directly does not put the idea in their head. It shows you can handle the answer, and it often brings relief.

4. Help them through the next few hours

Ask what's helped before. Help them use their safety plan. Move anything they could use to hurt themselves out of reach, if it's safe to.

5. Get help together

Offer to make the call with them: their GP, a helpline, or NHS 111 (press 2 in Wales and England).

Call 999 now if they've hurt themselves, have a plan and the means to act on it, or you can't keep them safe.

Look after yourself too

Supporting someone is heavy. Talk to someone you trust afterwards. Samaritans (116 123) are there for supporters too.

Send this to your person so they know what to do before they ever need it.
